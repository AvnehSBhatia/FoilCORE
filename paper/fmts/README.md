## Note on the label-leaking baseline

The label-leaking baseline (`--protocol gtmix`) is scheduled sampling under the full mask: the leak fraction q starts at 1 and falls by 0.03 every three epochs, reaching 0 at epoch 103. Early stopping on validation error (with drafts as input) decides where it ends. The kept checkpoints are from epochs 1–2 on ECG (q ≈ 1), 140–149 on PM2.5 (q = 0) and 4/79/88 on ETTh1. Its stream/smooth gap therefore comes mostly from the full mask on PM2.5 and ECG, and its leak penalty is confounded with training length. The fixed-q sweep (`train.py --protocol draft_dual --q Q`) is the clean test of leakage.
